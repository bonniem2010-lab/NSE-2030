How the call is made

Four rules each vote +1 (buy), 0 or −1 (sell): price against its 200-day average, the 50-day average against the 200-day, the three-month return (±10% counts), and the 14-day RSI (above 70 is overbought, below 30 oversold). A total of +2 or more is BUY, −2 or less is SELL, anything between is HOLD. Rules without enough history are skipped.

This is a rules-of-thumb technical screen for personal research, not financial advice. It ignores valuation, earnings and news.
